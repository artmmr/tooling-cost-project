Assumptions used
  • Complete-tool estimate: working components, construction hours, design, tool base and standard parts, assembly and tryout. Hot runners and machine-specific automation are not included.
  • Cost per part includes replacement working components when the quantity exceeds their typical life.
  • Labour and machine rates are the selected country's defaults.
  • Standard tolerance and surface finish, no extra corrosion or abrasion protection.
  • No cooling system and no transport cost.
  • European countries are costed in euros. China and India are costed in US dollars; EUR benchmarks are converted at the European Central Bank reference rate.
  • The range reflects the typical accuracy of an early estimate (−20% to +30%). It is not a statistical confidence interval.
