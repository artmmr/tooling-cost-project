Transport parameters

Distances are entered in Overview. Freight prices below apply to one vehicle or sea load. The estimate allocates them to the tooling weight, or charges whole loads.

Two charged journeys preserve the original estimate’s ×2 factor. Change this to match your shipment. Proportional allocation may differ from a carrier’s minimum charge.

Calculate to see road and sea freight costs.

Freight = distance × rate × load allocation × charged journeys. Freight amounts are shown before the estimate’s final 10% markup.
